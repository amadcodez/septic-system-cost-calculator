What to have ready before you call

Quotes get sharper when you can answer three questions: how many bedrooms the house has, what your soil is like (or whether a perc test exists), and whether this is new construction, a replacement, or a repair. Five minutes with the cost calculator arms you with the numbers a contractor will otherwise control.

How to compare septic quotes

Make every bid itemise the same four things: tank size and material, absorption area in square feet, system type, and who pulls the permit. A quote that is thousands cheaper is almost always one of those four being quietly smaller. Ask what happens if rock is found during excavation — a fixed price and an hourly rate are very different bids.

Checking a contractor properly

  • Licence for onsite/septic work in your state — most states license installers separately from general contractors, and your state page names the licensing agency.
  • Liability insurance, confirmed in writing.
  • Local references from the last year — soil is local, and a contractor who works your county knows what the inspector wants to see.
  • Who attends the inspection: the installer should be on site when the health department sees the open trenches.
